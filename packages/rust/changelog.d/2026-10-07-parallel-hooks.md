**Changed** An `on-file` command's runs now execute concurrently, one per CPU at most, when a table's paths outgrow one argument list, and each run's output is parsed as it is written instead of after it exits. Rows keep their order within a run but not across runs. A 2M-row JSONL ingest through `jq` runs within 5% of the equivalent `jq | awk` pipeline, and a frontmatter parse over thousands of files runs in a quarter of the time of `find -exec awk +`. The scratch database no longer syncs to disk and caches 64 MiB of pages.
