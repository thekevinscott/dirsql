**Fixed** Declared foreign keys are checked once per unit of work: the build, or one batch of watch events across all tables. A child row may land before its parent, and editing a referenced parent no longer fails. A row that references nothing when the unit ends fails the unit with the table, file and values named, instead of a bare `FOREIGN KEY constraint failed`; in watch mode the batch is rolled back, reported as one error event, and retried with later batches until one resolves it.
