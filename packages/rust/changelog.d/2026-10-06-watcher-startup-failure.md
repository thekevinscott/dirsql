**Fixed** On Linux, a watcher that fails to start because an inotify limit is exhausted now names that limit and the sysctl to raise: `fs.inotify.max_user_instances` for `Too many open files (os error 24)`, `fs.inotify.max_user_watches` for `OS file watch limit reached`. `dirsql server` now answers `GET /events` with `503` and `{"error": "filesystem watcher failed to start: …"}` when its watcher could not attach. Before, it sent `event: ready` and then no events.
