**Changed**

- **The scan progress line is drawn by `indicatif`.** The reporter's `\r`-rewritten counter, its 100 ms redraw throttle and its line-padding are gone, replaced by `indicatif`'s draw target at 10 Hz. `DIRSQL_PROGRESS` is untouched — `auto`, `always` and `never` mean exactly what they meant, the `auto` warmup is still half a second, percentages still floor, and a piped or redirected run under the default is still byte-for-byte silent (verified by diffing captured stdout and stderr across the change, not by reading `indicatif`'s docs). What a watcher sees differs in two ways: a redraw now clears the whole line width instead of only the tail of the line it replaces, and `indicatif`'s throttle allows a short burst before settling to 10 Hz, so a fast phase under `DIRSQL_PROGRESS=always` emits more frames than it used to. The drawn text carries no ANSI escapes, so a redirected `always` run stays readable in a file. Costs `indicatif` and `console` as dependencies of the published crate, which reverses the module's original "no new dependencies" note: a redrawn counter is exactly the infrastructure a real crate covers.
