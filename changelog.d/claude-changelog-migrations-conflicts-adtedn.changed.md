- **Changelog entries are now per-PR fragments under `changelog.d/` (#563, epic #561).** Instead of editing `CHANGELOG.md`'s shared `## [Unreleased]` section (which merge-conflicted with every other in-flight PR), each PR adds one `changelog.d/<branch-slug>.<category>.md` file. Sections are assembled into `CHANGELOG.md` by towncrier at release-notes time via the dispatch-triggered "Release Notes" workflow, which opens an assemble PR; the historical `## [Unreleased]` content is frozen as the dated `## [2026-07-09]` section. Consumers of `CHANGELOG.md` are unaffected.
